The IKO Shingle Benefit

At N & I Roofing, we believe in providing our customers with the best roofing solutions available, which is why we exclusively use IKO shingles.

🛡️

Superior Durability

IKO Dynasty Shingles have a Class 3 impact rating which offers much more resistance to hail and debris impact. IKO shingles are also rated for up to 130mph winds, ensuring that your roof will withstand harsh weather conditions.

🎨

Aesthetic Appeal

IKO shingles offer a large variety of bold colors with a deep shadow band that portrays a more textured and attractive roof. Dynasty series shingles are also algae resistant to prevent against discoloration and streaking over time.

📜

Warranty Protection

IKO Dynasty shingles are designed for longevity, coming with a limited lifetime warranty which covers manufacturing defects and ensures that your roof will be protected for the life of the product.

💰

Insurance Benefits

Because IKO Dynasty series shingles are so durable, some insurance companies offer discounts due to the shingles having a Class 3 impact rating. Contact your agent to see if you qualify.
